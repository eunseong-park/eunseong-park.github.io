## Heidelberg University

**Applied Equilibrium Analysis in Environmental and Energy Economics** (Master's level, Prof. Dr. Sebastian Rausch)

Teaching assistant with supervision of group projects (Summer 2023, Winter 2024/25, Winter 2025/26, Winter 2026/27; offered as *Quantitative Methods in Energy and Environmental Economics* in Summer 2023). Students analyze emissions reduction pathways in the global steel industry using partial equilibrium models, evaluating climate policy instruments (carbon taxes, ETS, technology standards) and the effectiveness of Border Carbon Adjustments in mitigating carbon leakage.

* **[Group Project Repository](https://github.com/eunseong-park/steel-decarbonization)**  
  Codebase with equivalent implementations in GAMS, Python (GAMSPy/Pyomo), and Julia (JuMP) for modeling heterogeneous steel plants and simulating climate policies.
